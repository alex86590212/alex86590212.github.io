Bachelor thesis · University of Groningen

CERBERUS: a neuro-symbolic safety gate for LLM agents

A safety layer that combines LLM-based reasoning with formal logic programming (Clingo/ASP) to constrain and validate autonomous agent behavior before it is executed.

Hybrid physics and ML

Satellite tracking and anomaly detection

SGP4 orbital propagation combined with LSTM, Transformer and Bayesian networks to learn residual orbital dynamics in Low Earth Orbit, with Bayesian uncertainty for risk-aware prediction. Label-free anomaly detection from prediction error, with strict temporal splits to prevent leakage.

Validated with Wilcoxon signed-rank, Cliff's delta and Holm–Bonferroni correction.

>98%
Position error reduction vs. physics-only

Reinforcement learning

HGT-JSSP: intersection scheduling

Vehicles crossing an unsignalized intersection, formulated as a Job Shop Scheduling Problem (vehicles as jobs, conflict zones as machines). A Heterogeneous Graph Transformer policy trained with PPO, a feasible-action mask and curriculum training.

Measured on synthetic scenarios: 49.0% reduction on the hardest tier.

−45.6%
Mean waiting time vs. iGreedy
